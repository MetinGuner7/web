What the app does

📋
Daily predictions
New football predictions every day — 1X2, over/under, both teams to score, correct score and more — each with its odds and a 1–10 confidence rating.
📊
A public record
Every settled prediction since 1 March 2026 stays on the record, wins and losses, with the win rate for each confidence band.
⭐
Tip of the day
The day's highest-confidence prediction, from the sports you choose to follow.
🧩
Ready-made combinations
Multi-match combinations built from the day's predictions. They are predictions to follow, not bets: the app takes no stakes and places no bets.
💬
AI explanations
An explanation written by AI from what the pick carries: the market, the odds and the confidence rating.
🌍
More sports
Football first, plus basketball, tennis, American football, ice hockey, baseball, MMA and e-sports, in 12 languages.
